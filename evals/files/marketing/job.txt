Growth Marketing Manager
Northlight Apps · Amsterdam, Netherlands (hybrid, three days a week in our Amsterdam office)

About Northlight
Northlight builds Wander, a trip-planning app used by 3 million travellers across Europe. We are a team of 120 and we believe great travel should be effortless, delightful and accessible to everyone.

The role
You will own paid acquisition and lifecycle marketing for Wander in our five core markets. You will plan and spend a yearly budget of more than €500K, run the experimentation programme across channels, and work closely with product and data to grow activated users profitably. You will report to the Head of Growth.

What you'll do
- Plan, launch and optimise paid campaigns on Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and Google Ads (including App campaigns)
- Own the creative testing roadmap with our in-house designers
- Run A/B tests across ads, app store pages and onboarding, and turn the results into decisions
- Build and improve lifecycle journeys (push, email, in-app) in Braze
- Report weekly on CAC, ROAS and payback using GA4 and Amplitude
- Work with the product and data teams on attribution and event tracking

What we're looking for
- 4+ years in performance or growth marketing, ideally for consumer mobile apps
- Hands-on experience with Meta Ads and Google Ads; TikTok Ads is a strong plus
- Experience owning a significant budget and reporting on ROAS and CAC
- A track record of structured experimentation (A/B testing)
- Comfortable with GA4 and a product analytics tool such as Amplitude or Mixpanel
- Basic SQL to pull your own data
- Fluent English; Dutch is a plus

Nice to have
- App store optimisation (ASO)
- Lifecycle marketing experience with Braze or a similar tool
- Experience in travel or marketplaces

What we offer
- Salary €70,000 to €85,000 plus equity
- Relocation support and a 30% ruling application where eligible
- 28 days of holiday and a yearly travel budget

Northlight is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants of every background.
