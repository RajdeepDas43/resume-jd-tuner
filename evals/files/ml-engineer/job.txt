Machine Learning Engineer, Search Ranking
Quillstone · Bengaluru, India (hybrid)

About us

What you will do

- Design offline evaluations (NDCG, MRR) and online A/B tests
- Partner with search infrastructure engineers on retrieval quality

You should have
- 3+ years building machine learning systems in production
- Strong Python and SQL
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Experience with ranking, recommendation or search relevance
- Experience running A/B tests and reading their results
- Experience deploying models on Kubernetes

Nice to have
- Elasticsearch or OpenSearch
- Embeddings and vector search (FAISS, Milvus)
- MLflow or a similar experiment tracker
- Spark
